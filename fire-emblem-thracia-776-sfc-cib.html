Fire Emblem: Thracia 776 (ファイアーエムブレム トラキア776 SFC Rare CIB)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays on Japanese Super Famicom and US SNES (with simple internal plastic tab removal).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Released in late 1999 and 2000 at the very death of the Super Famicom, Thracia 776 is the undisputed holy grail of the entire Fire Emblem franchise. Sold originally via Nintendo Power flash-ROM rewrite kiosks and later in tiny cartridge quantities, this masterclass in brutal tactical perfection is one of the rarest 16-bit games in history.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

ROM CHIP VERIFICATION & DELUXE BOX INTEGRITY: 1) Extreme bootleg risk: Cheap AliExpress repro carts flood the market. Confirm genuine Nintendo SHVC-8X PCB with mask ROM chip 'SHVC-B9XJ-JPN'. 2) Cartridge shell: Check for clean factory debossing. 3) Kiosk vs. Retail: Both the flash-ROM cartridge and retail boxed cart are highly sought-after.

💡 Insider Tip: Always insist on inspecting high-resolution photos of the internal PCB board contacts and screws before purchasing. Authentic Japanese retail boxed copies are blue-chip video game investments.
