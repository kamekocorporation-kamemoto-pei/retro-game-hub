Pokémon Emerald, Ruby & Sapphire (Authentic Japanese CIB)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free hardware compatibility (Note: Game text is in Japanese, highly prized by shiny hunters, speedrunners, and CIB collectors).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Over 80% of loose English Pokémon GBA cartridges on Western marketplaces are counterfeit. Japanese CIB (Complete in Box) copies are far cheaper, authentic, and feature stunning exclusive box artwork.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

HOW TO SPOT REAL JAPANESE CARTS: 1) Authentic Japanese Emerald uses a translucent emerald shell where you can see the internal battery and vertical golden rectangles on the back PCB. 2) Look for a two-digit factory number stamped (indented) into the front label. 3) Verify '純正品' (Authentic) and check that the original cardboard box includes the inner tray.

💡 Insider Tip: Many Japanese sellers specify '電池切れ' (Dry internal clock battery - game still saves, but time-based events stop) or '電池交換済' (New battery installed).
