1. Why Import from Japan?

The original launch-edition Japanese Sega Saturn features an iconic two-tone slate grey aesthetic with brilliant cyan and orange accent buttons that stands in stark contrast to the drab black Western shell. Model 1 units also feature dedicated disc-access and power LED indicators along with top-tier CD drive tracking assemblies.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

DRIVE REVISION & CAPACITOR AUDIT: 1) Model 1s have oval power/reset buttons. Ensure the tray motor opens smoothly and disc read speeds are snappy. 2) Power supply board: Check for any bulging capacitors near the PSU heatsink. 3) Internal clock battery: The CR2032 bay on the rear must be clean of corrosion or battery acid leaks.

💡 Insider Tip: Pair your Japanese Model 1 Saturn with an Action Replay 4M Plus cartridge to bypass region locks and unlock the necessary 4MB RAM expansion for Capcom's arcade fighting ports.
