Casio Loopy: My Seal Computer (SV-100 Console)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Japan-exclusive 32-bit console. Region-free for all Japanese Loopy game cartridges.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Released in October 1995 exclusively in Japan, the Casio Loopy holds a unique spot in video game history: it was marketed specifically to female gamers and featured a built-in full-color thermal sticker printer that printed instant stickers from in-game screenshots and custom avatar designers. It is an extraordinary historical piece of hardware engineering.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

THERMAL PRINTER MECHANISM & CARTRIDGE RECOGNITION: 1) Thermal printer head: Test that the internal printer feeder advances and the thermal head activates without error lights. 2) Casing: The soft pastel shell is prone to sun-fading on the cartridge door. 3) Controller: Confirm the single original gamepad with its oversized face buttons is included.

💡 Insider Tip: Standard 3M thermal sticker paper cartridges can be refilled. Look for listings bundled with 'Wanwan Aijou Monogatari' or 'Anime Land' and the optional video capture accessory (Magical Shop).
