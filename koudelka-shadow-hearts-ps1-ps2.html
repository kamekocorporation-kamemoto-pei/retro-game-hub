1. Why Import from Japan?

Created by former Squaresoft composers and designers under Sacnoth, Koudelka and its Shadow Hearts sequels blended gothic Lovecraftian horror with turn-based RPG mechanics and the iconic 'Judgment Ring' combat timing system. Japanese complete copies sell for a fraction of inflated Western eBay prices while retaining superior packaging.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

MULTI-DISC PS1 BLACK POLYCARBONATE & MANUALS: 1) Koudelka is a 4-disc PS1 epic: verify all 4 black-bottom discs have matching matrix codes and zero disc rot pinholes when held against light. 2) Shadow Hearts 1 & 2 on PS2 feature original Japanese cover art by Miyako Kato. 3) Spine cards: Verify authentic Obi cards are included for investment preservation.

💡 Insider Tip: Koudelka's cinematic cutscenes were recorded in native English by British Shakespearean actors—the Japanese release gives you the complete gothic horror experience without any language barrier!
