DoDonPachi & Dangun Feveron (怒首領蜂 & 弾銃フィーバロン Cave Shmups)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Sega Saturn / PS1 versions require Japanese NTSC-J hardware or Action Replay / FreePSXBoot.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Cave is the undisputed demigod of manic bullet-hell (Danmaku) shoot-'em-ups. Classics like DoDonPachi (怒首領蜂) and Dangun Feveron on Japanese Sega Saturn and PS1 feature incredible arcade vertical (TATE) display modes, thumping techno soundtracks, and thousands of neon bullets dancing across CRT scanlines.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

SPINE CARD (OBI) & DISC ROT CHECK: 1) Value driver: Cave shmups are prime collector targets; original spine cards (Obi) and registration postcards often double the set value. 2) Disc inspection: Hold discs up to a bright backlight to check for pinhole rot in the reflective aluminum layer. 3) Manual: High-gloss Cave manuals should show no staple rust.

💡 Insider Tip: Search '怒首領蜂 セガサターン 帯付' or '怒首領蜂 PS1'. Rotate your CRT television 90 degrees into TATE mode to experience pure, uncompressed arcade vertical bullet-hell perfection.
