1. Why Import from Japan?

Regarded by many retro critics as the greatest traditional Castlevania game ever made, Akumajō Dracula X: Chi no Rondo was released exclusively in Japan on PC Engine Super CD-ROM2 and never received a Western physical CD release!

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

SPOTTING FAKE CDR BOOTLEGS: 1) Authentic Konami CD discs (KMCD3005) have a deep mirror finish with crisp silk-screened artwork and an IFPI code on the inner plastic ring. Hold the disc up to the light: genuine pressed CDs are completely opaque, whereas pirate CDRs allow light through. 2) The manual is a thick, 28-page full-color booklet on glossy cardstock with Richter Belmont and Maria Renard character profiles. 3) Check for the authentic spine card ('帯' - Obi).

💡 Insider Tip: A complete copy with the original Obi spine card and registration card is an elite collector's grail that continues to appreciate steadily.
