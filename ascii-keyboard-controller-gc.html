ASCII Keyboard Controller for GameCube (Phantasy Star Online)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free (Works on all GameCube and Wii systems globally).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Created for Phantasy Star Online Episode I & II, this hybrid controller/keyboard is one of the most iconic and sought-after Nintendo peripherals ever made, and it was never sold in North American retail stores.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

No full replicas exist due to the complex mold, but verify that BOTH purple and grey dual plugs (controller port + keyboard port) are intact and uncut.

💡 Insider Tip: Keys often yellow over time; search with '美品' (Near Mint) or '箱付き' (Boxed) if you want crisp white keycaps.
