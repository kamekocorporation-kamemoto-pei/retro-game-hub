Lagrange Point (VRC7 FM Synth Chip) & Holy Diver (Famicom Collector Grails)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays on original Famicom or AV Famicom (Plays on NES with pinout adapter that supports expansion audio).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Konami's Lagrange Point contains the legendary VRC7 custom mapper chip, featuring a built-in 6-channel Yamaha YM2413 FM synthesizer that delivers mind-blowing 16-bit arcade synth music on an 8-bit Famicom console. Paired with Irem's hardcore dark-fantasy action platformer Holy Diver, they are pinnacle 8-bit collector grails.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

KONAMI VRC7 SOUND CHIP VERIFICATION: 1) Lagrange Point PCB: Open the cartridge to verify the genuine surface-mount 'KONAMI VRC7' audio chip. 2) Holy Diver: Authentic Irem cartridges use heavy textured grey plastic shells with the molded Irem logo and rear safety warnings. 3) Cardboard boxes: Verify the original Konami and Irem cardboard packaging contains the inner plastic tray and full-color instruction booklets.

💡 Insider Tip: To hear Lagrange Point's FM synthesis audio on a Western NES, ensure your 60-to-72 pin adapter has the expansion audio jumper wire connected to pin 54!
