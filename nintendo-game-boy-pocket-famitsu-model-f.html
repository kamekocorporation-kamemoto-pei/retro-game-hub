Game Boy Pocket Famitsu Model-F (Skeleton & Clear JDM Limited Editions)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free (Plays all original Game Boy mono cartridges worldwide on sharp FSTN display).

1. Why Import from Japan?

In collaboration with legendary Japanese gaming publication Weekly Famitsu, Nintendo produced the ultra-exclusive Game Boy Pocket 'Model-F' Skeleton edition. Featuring a smoky translucent front, solid black back, and exclusive Famitsu emblems, it is one of the most stylish Game Boys ever minted.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

AUTHENTIC FAMITSU BADGING & REAR HOUSING: 1) Front lens badge: Authentic Model-F units feature the distinctive white 'Famitsu: Entertainment Magazine' circular logo printed in the lower right corner of the glass screen lens. 2) Authentic two-tone housing: The front shell is smoky translucent acrylic, while the rear battery shell is solid matte black with original Japanese caution stickers. Beware of all-clear aftermarket shells with fake stickers! 3) Screen contrast: Inspect the silver FSTN LCD for screen bleeding or dead lines.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for listings specifying 'ファミ通限定 Model-F 当時物' to guarantee period authenticity and avoid Chinese replica shells.
