Capcom Steel Battalion 40-Button Giant Mecha Controller Set (Tekki JDM)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free! Works on any original Xbox console worldwide (or PC via USB adapter).

1. Why Import from Japan?

The most legendary specialized controller in video game history! Built by Capcom for 'Tekki' (Steel Battalion), this massive 3-piece cockpit controller spans nearly 3 feet across, boasting 40 lighted buttons, dual joysticks, a gear shifter, tuning dial, three metal foot pedals, and an illuminated emergency Eject button under an acrylic safety flap.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

THREE-SECTION CONNECTOR BLOCKS & FOOT PEDALS: 1) The controller connects via three modular interlocking blocks (Left, Center, Right); verify the connecting hex bolts and modular ribbon cables are included. 2) Metal Foot Pedals: Test all 3 foot pedals (Gas, Brake, Step) to ensure potentiometer tension springs are firm. 3) Eject Button: Verify the yellow Eject button safety cover opens smoothly and the internal incandescent bulb illuminates on ignition sequence.

💡 Insider Tip: First printings feature green button backlighting, while the rare second run features blue backlighting. Japanese boxed sets ('鉄騎 コントローラー 完品') are true museum showpieces.
