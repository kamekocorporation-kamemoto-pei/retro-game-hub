1. Why Import from Japan?

Affectionately dubbed the 'Green Goblin' by the competitive fighting game community, the Japanese Sega Dreamcast Arcade Stick (HKT-7300) features a rock-solid heavy metal base, striking green-and-grey aesthetic, and microswitch components engineered to withstand Marvel vs. Capcom 2 and Street Fighter III: 3rd Strike marathons.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

STICK RE-CENTERING & VMU SLOT PINS: 1) Stick tension: Ensure the stick snaps dead-center without loose wobble. 2) VMU slot: Look down the top slot with a flashlight to verify none of the VMU connector pins are bent or snapped. 3) Steel base plate: Verify rubber feet are present to prevent desktop sliding.
