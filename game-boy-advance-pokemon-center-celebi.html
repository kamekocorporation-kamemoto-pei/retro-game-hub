🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free (Plays all Game Boy, Game Boy Color, and Game Boy Advance cartridges globally).

1. Why Import from Japan?

The Japanese Pokemon Center retail stores released some of the most sought-after original AGB-001 handhelds in history: Celebi Green (translucent emerald green with Pikachu/Celebi silhouette), Suicune Blue, Latios/Latias, and New York Pokemon Center editions.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

AFTERMARKET RESHELL SPOTTING: Western marketplaces are saturated with counterfeit Celebi/Suicune shells: 1) Lens artwork: Authentic Pokemon Center lenses have laser-crisp character silhouettes with flawless screen-print alignment; aftermarket lens prints are blurry with dull ink. 2) Rear serial sticker: Authentic JDM units feature Japanese telecommunications badges with serial starting with 'AJ'; reproductions have fake English FCC text. 3) Shell seam quality: OEM Nintendo shells have rounded, silky matte seam lines, while fakes have sharp molding flash around the battery door.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for '当時物 純正シェル' (Period original OEM shell) and verify the original cardboard box with matching Pokemon Center barcodes.
