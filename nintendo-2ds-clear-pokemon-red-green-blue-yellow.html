Nintendo 2DS Clear Colorway Series (Pokemon Red, Green, Blue & Yellow JDM Bundles)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Region-locked to Japanese 3DS/DS cartridges (Can be made region-free in 5 minutes via Luma3DS custom firmware).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Released exclusively in Japan to celebrate the 20th Anniversary of Pokemon, these translucent wedge 2DS consoles match the original 1996 Game Boy cartridge colors: Clear Red, Clear Green, Clear Blue, and Clear Yellow. Featuring colored face buttons and iconic big-box packaging with classic maps, they are prime collector targets.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

ORIGINAL SPECIAL EDITION BOX & MAP INCLUSIONS: 1) Authentic bundles must include the original big cardboard display box featuring the nostalgic 1996 Ken Sugimori town map poster and original stickers. 2) The translucent casing plastic has a distinct satin finish; verify the back serial sticker matches the serial barcode printed on the warranty paperwork. 3) Circle Pad: Verify the rubber thumbstick grip has not degraded or torn.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for listings stating 'タウンマップ付属 完品' (Town Map Included / Complete in Box) to secure full investment potential.
