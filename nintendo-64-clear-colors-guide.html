Nintendo 64 Jusco & Clear Color Consoles (NUS-001 JPN)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Hardware is identical to North American N64! Only a plastic cartridge tray tab separates US and JP carts (easily swapped with a region-free 3D printed tray in 2 minutes).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Japan received exclusive two-tone clear consoles (Midnight Blue, Clear Blue/White, Clear Red/White, and the rare Jusco 30th Anniversary Clear Grey) at far lower prices than US Funtastic models.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

Watch out for cheap translucent AliExpress replacement shells. Genuine Japanese N64 consoles have original serial number metal plates inside the bottom slot and authentic 'NUS-001(JPN)' stickers.

💡 Insider Tip: Check if the console includes the '拡張パック' (Memory Expansion Pak - NUS-007) under the front jumper lid—many Japanese bundles include it for free!
