PlayStation Vita Slim (PCH-2000 JDM Exclusive Colors)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free with Full English OS support right out of the box.

1. Why Import from Japan?

North America only received standard Black and Aqua Blue. Japan received Neon Orange, Glacier White, Lime Green/White, Light Blue, Metallic Red, and Dragon Quest Metal Slime editions.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

Beware of third-party refurbished units from China using cheap replacement LCDs. Japanese domestic listings ('国内版') with original box ('箱付き') guarantee genuine Sony parts and original serial labels.

💡 Insider Tip: In Japanese PS Vita menus, the 'O' button is Confirm and 'X' is Cancel. You can easily swap this or change system language to English during initial setup.
