Kiki Kaikai: Nazo no Kuro Manto & Tsukiyo Soushi (Pocky & Rocky 1 & 2 SFC CIB)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays on Super Famicom or SNES with modified cartridge slot. Minimal text barrier (Cute shrine maiden co-op shooter).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Known in the West as Pocky & Rocky, Natsume's shrine-maiden co-op action shooters are two of the most beloved games on the Super Nintendo. While Western copies sell for exorbitant sums, the Japanese original Super Famicom releases feature superior Japanese folklore artwork and pristine CIB preservation at a fraction of the cost.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

NATSUME / TAITO PCB VERIFICATION: 1) Authentic boards use official Nintendo SHVC-KN (Kiki Kaikai 1) and SHVC-K2 (Kiki Kaikai 2) mask ROMs. 2) Factory stamp: Check for the two-digit indented factory stamp on the front label. 3) Box: The authentic cardboard box features bright, saturated pastel anime artwork of Sayo-chan and Manuke the Tanuki.

💡 Insider Tip: Both games feature full 2-player cooperative gameplay, making them absolute essentials for retro game night setups.
