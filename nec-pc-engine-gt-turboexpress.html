NEC PC Engine GT / TurboExpress (PI-TG6 Portable Console)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays all Japanese PC Engine HuCards natively. North American TurboGrafx HuCards require an internal pinout switch.

1. Why Import from Japan?

The PC Engine GT is the Ferrari of 90s handhelds: a portable unit that directly played full-sized, uncompromised home console HuCard games on an active-matrix color LCD back in 1990. North American TurboExpress units routinely fetch $500–$800, whereas sourcing a Japanese GT offers identical, flawless architecture at far more competitive pricing.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

RECAP AUDIT & TV TUNER BUS: 1) Extreme capacitor vulnerability: Like the Duo, unserviced GTs have dead sound and black screens due to leaking micro-capacitors. Only buy listings stating 'コンデンサ交換済・音声映像確認済' (recapped, sound/video verified). 2) HuCard lock slider: Ensure the card lock mechanism slides securely without cracking.

💡 Insider Tip: Only purchase from reputable Japanese retro modders who provide high-resolution photos of the refurbished motherboard and clear demonstration of audio playback from the internal speaker.
