1. Why Import from Japan?

Because the game text can be switched to English in the options menu, the Japanese release (SHVC-RI) is the single best kept secret in retro gaming: you get the authentic 1994 masterpiece in stunning CIB packaging for a fraction of US prices.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

REAL NINTENDO SHVC-RI PCB: 1) Authentic boards use original Nintendo MX mask ROMs (SHVC-RI-0) and gold-plated edge connectors. 2) The front label has a deep 2-digit indented factory stamp on the lower right corner. 3) The cardboard box features high-gloss printing with Samus Aran battling Ridley—avoid flat reproduction boxes with blurry text on the back barcodes.

💡 Insider Tip: Turn on the game, enter the Options menu, and select 'LANGUAGE: ENGLISH' to play the full game completely in English without any patching!
