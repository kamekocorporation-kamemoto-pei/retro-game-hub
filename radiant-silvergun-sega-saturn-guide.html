Radiant Silvergun (Sega Saturn - Treasure's Shmup Masterpiece)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Menus, HUD, and voice acting are in English! NTSC-J region bypassable via Action Replay 4M Plus cartridge.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Treasure's magnum opus vertical shooter was a Japanese Saturn exclusive. With mind-bending 7-weapon tactical mechanics, Hitoshi Sakimoto's orchestral soundtrack, and gorgeous 2D/3D anime cutscenes, it is the holy grail of Saturn shmups.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

CRITICAL SEGA WATERMARK & OBI CARD: Radiant Silvergun is one of the most pirated Saturn games in the world. 1) Inspect the underside mirror ring of the CD: authentic retail discs feature the repeating etched 'SEGA' holographic ring watermark and IFPI mastering code. Silver-backed CDRs or printed discs with solid inner rings are fake repros. 2) Authentic spine cards ('帯' - Obi) feature crisp blue/white text on thick cardstock, not inkjet photo paper.

💡 Insider Tip: A copy with authentic Obi spine card ('帯付') commands a 40% higher resale valuation and represents the pinnacle of shmup collecting.
