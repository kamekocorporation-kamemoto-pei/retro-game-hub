Victor V-Saturn (RG-JX1 & RG-JX2 JVC Licensed Sega Saturn Revisions)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% compatible with all Sega Saturn software and peripherals worldwide (Region-free with Action Replay 4M).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Under official license from Sega, electronics giant Victor (JVC) manufactured their own premium Saturn hardware: the RG-JX1 (matching Model 1 grey with purple buttons) and RG-JX2 (Model 2 with dark grey/champagne accents). Featuring upgraded video circuitry and unique Victor startup splash screens, they are high-tier collector variants.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

VICTOR BADGING & STARTUP BIOS LOGO: 1) The CD drive lid features the embossed gold/silver 'Victor' corporate logo instead of 'SEGA'. 2) Powering on the console must display the unique blue Victor orb startup sound animation, not standard Sega splash screens. 3) Rear model plate: Must read 'MODEL RG-JX1' or 'MODEL RG-JX2' manufactured by Victor Company of Japan. 4) Ensure the controller has the embossed Victor script logo.

💡 Insider Tip: RG-JX2 represents the most reliable Victor revision with late-production motherboards and long-life optical drive assemblies.
