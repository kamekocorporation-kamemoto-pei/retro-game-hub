Shining Force III Trilogy: Scenarios 1, 2, 3 & Premium Disc (Sega Saturn)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Requires Japanese NTSC-J Sega Saturn console or Action Replay / Pseudo Saturn cartridge.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Camelot's Shining Force III is one of the crowning achievements of tactical RPG history. While the West only received Scenario 1, Japan received the full, mind-blowing epic: Scenario 2 (Medion), Scenario 3 (Julian), and the legendary mail-away 'Premium Disc'. Sourcing the full Japanese 4-disc set is the only way to complete the grand narrative.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

PREMIUM DISC SLEEVE & SPINE CARDS (OBI): 1) Premium Disc: The mail-in Premium Disc came in a unique cardboard sleeve; verify the disc has zero scratches. 2) Spine cards (Obi): Pristine collector copies retain the original paper spine cards for all three volumes. 3) Disc pinholes: Hold discs up to light to verify no reflective layer rot.

💡 Insider Tip: English translation patch projects (Shining Force III Translation Project) allow seamless play using the original Japanese retail discs via Fenrir ODE or patched backups.
