Cotton 2 & Cotton Boomerang (Success Cute-'Em-Up Sega Saturn Grails)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Zero language barrier (High-energy magical shoot-'em-up arcade gameplay). Region-free with Action Replay 4M.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Developed by Success, Cotton 2 and its ultra-rare remix Cotton Boomerang are among the most revered and valuable 2D shooters on the Sega Saturn. Combining fighting-game command inputs with magical projectile chains, their physical Japanese releases are legendary collector grails.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

HIGH-VALUATION CDR BOOTLEG WARNING: Cotton Boomerang is frequently counterfeited: 1) Inner disc ring: Authentic retail pressed CDs have clean, laser-etched IFPI mastering matrix numbers on the mirror hub—never burned silver or greenish CDR dye. 2) Manual cardstock: Authentic manuals feature thick glossy cardstock with vivid candy-colored printing; reproductions have flat, slightly fuzzy text on the back barcodes. 3) Authentic '帯' (Obi card): Must have clean die-cut edges without inkjet bleeding.

💡 Insider Tip: Cotton Boomerang optionally supports the Sega Saturn 4MB RAM cart for extra sound effects and smoother frame rates—a crown jewel for any shmup collection.
