1. Why Import from Japan?

Developed by Treasure and Nintendo, Sin and Punishment is arguably the single greatest action shooter on the Nintendo 64. Released exclusively in Japan in late 2000, the game actually features native, full English voice acting out of the box! Japanese CIB copies with the stunning foil-accented box art are absolute collector essentials.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

BOX FOIL GLOSS & CARTRIDGE EMBOSS CHECK: 1) Outer box: Authentic Japanese boxes have a distinctive high-gloss finish with vibrant red-and-gold character art. 2) Cartridge rear label: Confirm two-digit numeric factory punch stamp on the back label. 3) PCB board: Authentic Treasure boards feature clean gold contacts and official Nintendo NUS-06A-01 ROM chips.
