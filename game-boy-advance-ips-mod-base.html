1. Why Import from Japan?

The original horizontal Game Boy Advance (AGB-001) is unanimously considered the most comfortable handheld ergonomic design Nintendo ever engineered, but its reflective screen is notoriously dark. Japanese imports are renowned for pristine motherboard PCB condition and immaculate button membranes, making them the world's premier candidates for modern IPS / ITA screen upgrades.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

POWER SWITCH & SHOULDER SWITCH INSPECTION: 1) Power switch contact: Dust accumulation causes random resets or dim red power LEDs even with fresh batteries; verify smooth power operation. 2) L/R microswitches: Check that tactical clicks are snappy. 3) Motherboard corrosion: Japanese units stored in dry closet environments rarely show battery acid rot.
