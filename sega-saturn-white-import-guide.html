Japanese Sega Saturn (White Console & 2D Shmups/Fighters)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Region-locked by default, but instantly unlocked for all regions using a simple Action Replay 4M Plus or Pseudo Saturn Kai cartridge.

1. Why Import from Japan?

The Sega Saturn was a massive hit in Japan, resulting in the legendary Mist Grey/White console design and hundreds of arcade-perfect 2D shooters and Capcom/SNK fighters that never left Japan.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

When buying high-end Japanese Saturn games (like Radiant Silvergun or Castlevania), always check for the '帯' (Obi spine card) and verify the Sega Saturn ring watermark on the bottom inner ring of the CD to avoid CDR reproductions.

💡 Insider Tip: Model HST-3220 (White Saturn) is the most reliable late-revision board. Always use an Action Replay 4M RAM cart to play X-Men vs. Street Fighter and Marvel Super Heroes.
