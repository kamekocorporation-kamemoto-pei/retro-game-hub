MOTHER 2: Gyiyg no Gyakushū / EarthBound (Super Famicom CIB)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays on Super Famicom or North American SNES with modified cartridge slot. (Text in Japanese; celebrated collector centerpiece with iconic pop-art box).

1. Why Import from Japan?

An authentic English SNES EarthBound box and cartridge sells for $1,500–$2,500+ and is 90% bootlegged on Western sites. The Japanese Super Famicom original release sells for $40–$70 in pristine CIB condition with iconic red box artwork designed by Shigesato Itoi!

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

AUTHENTICATING SHVC-MB MOTHER 2: 1) The cartridge uses model 'SHVC-MB' with a glossy red/black split front label containing an indented 2-digit factory stamp (e.g., '22' or '00'). 2) The gold Super Famicom seal on the back has sharp, metallic foil sheen. 3) The authentic red cardboard box contains the inner plastic tray, manual, and the fold-out town map flyer.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for listings stating 'マップ付き' (With fold-out map) or 'アンケートハガキ付き' (With registration postcard) for investment-grade copies.
