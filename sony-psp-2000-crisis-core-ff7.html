PSP-2000 Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII 10th Anniversary Limited Numbered Edition

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free for games! System OS switches to English natively in system settings.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Limited to precisely 77,777 individually numbered units exclusively in Japan, this iconic PSP-2000 features a lustrous silver metallic finish with Tetsuya Nomura's character artwork etched on the back and Buster Sword key-strap accessories.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

SERIAL NUMBER ENGRAVING & UMD DOOR LOGO: 1) Inspect the top rim of the console: authentic units feature a laser-etched serial number from '00001' to '77777' on the top plastic edge. Beware of aftermarket Chinese replacement shells that lack this laser engraving or use generic printed text. 2) UMD door ring: The silver ring features the crisp black silhouette of Cloud Strife and Zack Fair. 3) Battery: Inspect the original silver battery cover to ensure tight latching without swollen lithium battery cell damage.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for CIB sets that include the Buster Sword pewter keychain charm ('バスターソード ストラップ') to guarantee full collector value.

How to Choose Between the Big 3 Japanese Stores:

  • Suruga-ya (駿河屋): Japan's largest dedicated pre-owned hobby shop chain. Every console and cartridge is physically inspected, tested, and graded by store clerks before listing. You pay a slight store markup, but the risk of buying a counterfeit or modded fake is virtually zero. Best for high-end boxed (CIB) games and collector hardware.
  • Mercari Japan (メルカリ): Japan's #1 peer-to-peer flea market app where local gamers sell directly from their collections. Prices are 40% to 70% cheaper than western eBay. However, because everyday people list items, rare aftermarket reshells or reproduction carts can appear—which is why you should always compare listing photos with our Anti-Bootleg Verification Checklist above before buying!
  • Hard Off Net Mall (ハードオフ オフモール): The online catalog for Japan's beloved brick-and-mortar thrift chain with hundreds of stores nationwide. Each shop catalogs and photographs its own inventory.
