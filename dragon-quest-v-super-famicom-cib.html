Dragon Quest V: Tenkū no Hanayome (ドラゴンクエストV SFC CIB with Map)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays on Japanese Super Famicom or US SNES (with tray tab clip). Requires basic Japanese reading.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Dragon Quest V is a cultural landmark: a multi-generational epic where you grow from child to father, choose a bride, and tame wild monsters years before Pokémon existed. Sourcing the pristine Super Famicom original in its vibrant Toriyama-illustrated box complete with the fold-out world map is the quintessential Japanese RPG collector grail.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

MAP INCLUSION & SRAM BATTERY VOLTAGE: 1) World Map: Ensure the fold-out world map (ワールドマップ) is included and has clean, crisp fold lines. 2) Battery save: Original CR2032 batteries are now 30+ years old; verify the seller tested 'セーブ機能確認済' (save working) or replaced the battery. 3) Outer box: No crushed corners or sticker tears.

💡 Insider Tip: Because millions of copies were cherished in Japan, pristine complete-in-box copies can still be acquired for under $35. Search for 'ドラゴンクエストV スーパーファミコン 箱説マップ付 美品'.
