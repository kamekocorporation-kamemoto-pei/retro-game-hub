Fujitsu FM Towns Marty (First 32-Bit Home Console & Floppy Drive Belt Check)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Japanese domestic exclusive (Plays all FM Towns CD-ROM games and floppy software natively in NTSC).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Launched in 1993 as the world's very first 32-bit CD-ROM home console, the FM Towns Marty was sold strictly in Japan. Powered by an AMD 386SX processor, it plays legendary arcade-perfect ports of Tatsujin Ou, Street Fighter II Turbo, and iconic Japanese visual novels with redbook CD audio and rich 256-color sprite graphics.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

FLOPPY DRIVE BELT & CAPACITOR VERIFICATION: 1) Internal 3.5" Floppy Drive: 95% of unrestored Marty consoles have a dissolved internal floppy drive rubber drive belt ('フロッピードライブ不動'). Look for 'FDDベルト交換済' (Floppy belt replaced) or 'FDD読み込み確認済'. 2) SMD Capacitors: The mainboard audio circuitry requires recap inspection to avoid buzzing audio. 3) Verify the original two-tone grey/teal Marty controller with microswitched D-pad is included.

💡 Insider Tip: If buying a unit with broken floppy drive for cheap, replacement rubber belts cost under $5 and restore full save-game functionality!
