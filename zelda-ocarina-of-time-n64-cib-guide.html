The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Nintendo 64 JDM CIB V1.0)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% NTSC compatible! Swap the cartridge shell or use a region-free cartridge tray to play on North American N64s.

1. Why Import from Japan?

The Japanese release (NUS-CZLJ-JPN) features Shigeru Miyamoto's original vision, iconic Japanese typography on the golden foil box art, and pristine mint cardboard condition for 1/3 the cost of beat-up US grey/gold carts.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

CHECK REVISION & MOTHERBOARD PINS: 1) Early Japanese retail printings are Version 1.0 (NUS-CZLJ-JPN on the front label), containing the original unedited red blood and chant in the Fire Temple! 2) Check for the 2-digit indented factory stamp on the rear label. 3) Authentic N64 cartridge edge pins are gold-plated on a brown/tan PCB substrate, not bright green PCB material seen on Chinese counterfeit carts.

💡 Insider Tip: Check the listing for 'チラシ付' (Promotional flyer included) or '初期版' (Version 1.0 unedited release) for maximum historical value.
