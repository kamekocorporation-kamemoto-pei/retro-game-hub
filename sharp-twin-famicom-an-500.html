Sharp Twin Famicom (AN-500 Series Integrated Console & FDS Belt)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays all Japanese Famicom cartridge games AND Famicom Disk System disk cards natively in one unified console.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Manufactured under license by Sharp, the Twin Famicom combines the standard Famicom and Famicom Disk System into one sleek console, eliminating external power bricks and clumsy RAM adapter cables. Available in striking Black/Red and Red/Black two-tone editions.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

FLOPPY DRIVE RUBBER BELT & CONTROLLER INTEGRITY: 1) The FDS disk drive belt inside Sharp Twin units deteriorates into sticky black tar over 30 years. Verify 'FDDベルト交換済' (Floppy belt replaced) and 'ディスク読込動作確認済' (Disk read verified). 2) Player 2 controller: Inspect the built-in microphone and volume slider on Controller II—ensure it registers inputs. 3) Cartridge/Disk selector switch: Ensure the front mechanical slider switch clicks firmly between 'Cartridge' and 'Disk' modes.

💡 Insider Tip: The AN-505 late revision introduced Turbo fire buttons on the built-in controllers—look for 'AN-505-BK' or 'AN-505-RD' for the ultimate Famicom playing experience!
