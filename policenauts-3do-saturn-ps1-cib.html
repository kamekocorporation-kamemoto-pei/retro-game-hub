1. Why Import from Japan?

Following Snatcher, Hideo Kojima wrote and directed Policenauts, an astonishing cinematic sci-fi police drama that was never officially localized in the West. Sourcing the Japanese 3DO, Saturn, or PS1 releases grants you the complete boxed physical masterwork, accompanied by the iconic pilot handbook, art booklet, and CD slipcase.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

PILOT HANDBOOK & THICK BOX PACKAGING: 1) Inclusions: Check that the set includes all 3 discs, the thick instruction manual, and the special 'Pilot Handbook' guide. 2) Discs: Confirm no disc rot or surface scratching. 3) 3DO advantage: The 3DO release is completely region-free and plays directly on any 3DO console worldwide.
