Capcom Power Stick Fighter (CPSF-01 Super Famicom & Mega Drive Arcade Joystick)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free! Interchangeable detachable cable plugs into Super Famicom / SNES or Sega Mega Drive / Genesis.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Engineered directly by Capcom's arcade division for Street Fighter II Turbo, this heavyweight tabletop joystick features authentic arcade microswitches, individual turbo toggle sliders for all 6 buttons, and detachable controller interface cables for both Nintendo and Sega 16-bit consoles.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

MICROSWITCH CLICK & CABLE CONTINUITY: 1) Authentic sticks feature model 'CPSF-01' with the iconic Capcom yellow/blue branding. 2) Move the joystick in full 360-degree circles to verify all 4 arcade microswitches click loudly with instantaneous spring return. 3) Detachable cable: Verify the multi-pin DIN interface cable has no broken locking pins.

💡 Insider Tip: Search for listings stating 'SFC用ケーブル付属 動作確認済' to ensure the Super Famicom cable is present and tested.
