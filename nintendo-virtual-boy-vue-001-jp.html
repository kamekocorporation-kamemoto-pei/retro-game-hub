Nintendo Virtual Boy (VUE-001 Japanese Boxed Console & Eye-Shade Check)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free hardware! Plays North American and Japanese cartridges natively without any adapter.

1. Why Import from Japan?

While US Virtual Boy units frequently suffer from damaged boxes and missing stands, Japanese boxed units (VUE-001) were preserved in museum-grade condition. Japanese retail copies of iconic titles like Virtual Bowling, Space Invaders Virtual Collection, and SD Gundam Dimension War sell for significantly lower collector entry points than US releases.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

GLUE DEGRADATION & EYEPIECE HORIZONTAL LINES: 1) The #1 failure point on all Virtual Boy units is the LED display ribbon cable: factory adhesive glue dries up, causing horizontal line dropouts ('片目映らない' - one eye dead or glitching). Verify the seller has performed solder-bonding mod ('ハンダ修正済') or confirm both red LED arrays display crisp, zero-line-drop graphics. 2) Check the neoprene eye-shade visor: original foam disintegrates over 30 years; ensure the hard plastic eye-shade clip bracket ('アイシェードホルダー') is not cracked. 3) Verify the original adjustable zinc-alloy tripod stand ('スタンド') retains tight tension thumbscrews.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for listings stating '両目点灯動作確認済' (Both eye displays verified working). If buying as-is ('ジャンク'), factor in a $30 display ribbon solder-fix service.
