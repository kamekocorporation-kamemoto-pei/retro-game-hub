Game Boy Player & Startup Disc for GameCube

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: The bottom hardware attachment (DOL-017) is 100% Region-Free! (The Startup Disc itself is region-locked to Japanese consoles or modded systems).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Japan got exclusive matching colors (Spice Orange, Symphonic Green, Platinum, Indigo), and even if you use a US/PAL GameCube with Swiss homebrew, the Japanese hardware attachment fits and works natively.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

The Game Boy Player hardware cannot be easily faked, but buyers often accidentally purchase the unit WITHOUT the Startup Disc. Watch out for '本体のみ' (Unit only, no disc) vs. 'スタートアップディスク付' (Includes Startup Disc).

💡 Insider Tip: If you use GBI (Game Boy Interface) via SD Media Launcher on a Western GameCube, you only need the cheap Japanese hardware unit ('本体のみ') in your favorite color!
