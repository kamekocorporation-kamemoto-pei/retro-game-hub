Sega Fighting Pad 6B (SJ-6000 Official Japanese 6-Button Mega Drive Controller)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free! Plugs into any Sega Mega Drive, Genesis, or Master System console globally.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Widely hailed by fighting game purists as the greatest 2D D-pad ever crafted, the Japanese Fighting Pad 6B (SJ-6000) is slightly more compact and ergonomic than its bulky Western counterpart, featuring ultra-smooth floating directional pivots and instantaneous face-button action.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

COUNTERFEIT REPRODUCING PADS FLOOD EBAY: 1) Authentic Sega pads have model 'SJ-6000' molded on the rear casing with the official Sega debossed logo on the front face. 2) D-pad pivot: The authentic floating D-pad rocks smoothly on an internal central pivot dome; cheap Chinese reproductions feel stiff, gritty, and misregister diagonal inputs. 3) Mode button: The top-right shoulder 'MODE' button must click cleanly.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for multi-packs labeled 'セガ純正 6ボタンパッド SJ-6000 メンテ済' for authentic tournament-grade Street Fighter II and YuYu Hakusho play.
