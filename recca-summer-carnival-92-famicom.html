Summer Carnival '92: Recca (烈火 Naxat Soft Famicom Shoot-'Em-Up PCB Check)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% playable on Famicom or modified NES. Menus and score modes are in English!

1. Why Import from Japan?

Programmed by Shinobu Yagawa for the 1992 Naxat Summer Carnival competition, Recca pushed the Famicom hardware beyond its theoretical limits, displaying hundreds of bullet sprites at 60fps with heart-pounding techno music. Only roughly 5,000 cartridges were manufactured, making authentic copies legendary collector trophies.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

NAXAT SOFT PCB & FACTORY STAMP INSPECTION: 1) Extreme bootleg risk: Thousands of cheap Chinese reproduction carts flood the market: open the cartridge to verify genuine Nintendo PCB traces with original mask ROMs labeled 'NX9201'. Any board with modern flash chips or glob-top epoxy blobs is a reproduction. 2) Label: The authentic front label has a crisp fiery metallic orange sheen with an indented 2-digit factory stamp.

💡 Insider Tip: Recca's score attack and time attack modes are the gold standard of 8-bit competitive shooting—an authentic copy is a blue-chip retro investment.
