1. Why Import from Japan?

The US Dreamcast release of MvC2 sells for $150–$250+, while the original Japanese release (T-1212M) features the exact same tournament-standard Naomi arcade port, English voice lines, and gorgeous Capcom collector artwork for under $60!

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

HIGH-DENSITY GD-ROM INNER RING: 1) Authentic Dreamcast discs are GD-ROMs, NOT standard CD-ROMs. Look at the data side: you will see two distinct data rings (a narrow inner track and a wide high-density outer track) separated by a clear mirror gap containing etched Sega copyright text. 2) Repro bootlegs are burned on standard single-density CD-Rs or MIL-CDs.

💡 Insider Tip: Always verify '帯付き' (With Obi spine card) to secure an investment-grade Capcom fighting game collectible.
