MOTHER 3 (Game Boy Advance Authentic Japanese Release)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free (Japanese text; beloved by EarthBound collectors and compatible with cartridge dumpers for English fan-translation patching).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Never released outside Japan, Mother 3 is the crown jewel of any GBA collection. While eBay is flooded with $15 fake English repro carts, the authentic Japanese boxed release holds real collector value.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

CRITICAL CHECK: Almost every loose 'English Mother 3' cart online is a cheap Chinese bootleg that corrupts save files. A real Mother 3 cartridge ONLY exists in Japanese (model AGB-B3UJ-JPN) in a clean red cardboard box.

💡 Insider Tip: For the ultimate grail, search for the 'MOTHER3 デラックスボックス' (Deluxe Box), which includes the limited edition Mother 3 Game Boy Micro and Franklin Badge.
