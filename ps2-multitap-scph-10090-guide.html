Official PlayStation 2 Multitap (SCPH-10090 Fat vs. SCPH-70120 Slim)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free! Compatible with all global PS2 consoles for legendary 4-player party games like TimeSplitters 2 and Def Jam: Fight for NY.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Official Sony OEM Multitaps are virtually indestructible and sell in Japan for under $10, but Western buyers must know the critical difference between Fat and Slim models.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

FAT VS. SLIM PLUG SPACING DIFFERENCE: 1) **SCPH-10090**: Designed strictly for 'Fat' PS2 models (SCPH-10000 to 50000). The controller and memory card plugs are fixed and will NOT fit into Slim PS2 consoles! 2) **SCPH-70120**: Designed strictly for 'Slim' PS2 consoles (SCPH-70000 to 90000). 3) Beware of cheap generic third-party multitaps, which frequently fail to support DualShock 2 analog pressure-sensitive buttons.

💡 Insider Tip: Double-check your PS2 console model: if you have a Fat console, buy 'SCPH-10090'; if you have a Slim, buy 'SCPH-70120'.
