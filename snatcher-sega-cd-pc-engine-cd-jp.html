1. Why Import from Japan?

Hideo Kojima's cyberpunk masterpiece Snatcher on English Sega CD is a multi-thousand-dollar unobtainable relic. The definitive Japanese PC Engine Super CD-ROM2 and Sega Saturn releases feature the complete, uncensored story, glorious redbook synth-wave audio, and iconic anime cutscenes at a sensible price point.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

MANUAL THICKNESS & DISC DATA INTEGRITY: 1) Manual: The PC Engine release includes an incredible, thick comic-book style manual; ensure no moisture crinkling. 2) Disc condition: Verify both game data tracks and audio CD tracks load without stuttering on the title track. 3) Case sticker: Check for original Konami seal.
