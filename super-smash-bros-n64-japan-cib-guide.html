🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% NTSC compatible. English announcer and character voices are identical to the US release!

1. Why Import from Japan?

The Japanese original release (ニンテンドウオールスター! 大乱闘スマッシュブラザーズ) features exclusive sound effects (like heavier, punchier hit impacts), Japanese cartoon comic box art, and sells for a third of the inflated US market price.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

VERIFY ORIGINAL SOUND CLIP DIFFERENCES & PCB: 1) The Japanese version (NUS-NALJ-JPN) has different hit sounds and unique character victory animations. 2) Check the rear label for the factory-embossed stamp numbers. 3) The authentic box has a glossy, highly saturated cartoon cover featuring Pikachu, Mario, Link, and Kirby in hand-drawn style.

💡 Insider Tip: Competitive Smash 64 players frequently prefer the Japanese release because the hitstun and sound design are more satisfying in tournament play!
