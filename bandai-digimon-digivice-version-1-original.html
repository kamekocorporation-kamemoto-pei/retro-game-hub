1. Why Import from Japan?

Before the worldwide anime phenomenon, Bandai released the original 1997 Digital Monster virtual pet in Japan (Version 1 through 5) featuring classic brick-textured translucent plastic casings. Japanese editions feature exclusive evolutions, legendary sprite artwork, and high-value anime Digivice replicas.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

SPOTTING MODERN BOOTLEG V-PETS: 1) Authentic Bandai V-Pets feature crisp molded 'BANDAI 1997' copyright text on the battery door and authentic metal battle connector prongs on top. Counterfeits have flimsy metal contacts that misalign during battle. 2) Screen contrast: Authentic monochrome LCD screens have distinct pixel grid definitions and high contrast under natural light. 3) The authentic metal key-clip chain features solid link welding.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for '当時物 1997 バンダイ デジタルモンスター' (Period original 1997 Bandai) and check that the internal piezo buzzer beeps clearly during calls.
