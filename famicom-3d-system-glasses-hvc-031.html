Famicom 3D System (HVC-031 Active Shutter Glasses & Scope)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Requires an original Famicom or AV Famicom; works exclusively with CRT televisions.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Years before the Virtual Boy, Nintendo launched true stereoscopic 3D gaming in 1987 with the Japan-exclusive Famicom 3D System. Using active liquid crystal shutter glasses synchronized to CRT scanlines via the HVC-032 adapter box, games like 3D Hot Rally, Falsion, and Attack Animal Gakuen deliver jaw-dropping depth of field.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

SHUTTER LCD SYNCHRONIZATION & CRACKING: 1) Liquid crystal shutters: Check for dark black fluid leakage or cracks in the glass lenses. 2) Relay adapter box: Inspect the 15-pin Famicom expansion connector pins for bends. 3) IMPORTANT: Active shutter 3D only works on authentic 15kHz CRT monitors; modern LCDs cannot synchronize.

💡 Insider Tip: Always confirm the seller tested with a game like 'Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally' on a CRT TV. Units in original boxes with the foam tray keep the fragile glasses frame safe.
