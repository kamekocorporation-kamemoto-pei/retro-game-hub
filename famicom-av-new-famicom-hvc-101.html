AV Famicom / New Famicom (HVC-101 Console)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays 60-pin Japanese Famicom cartridges natively. 72-pin NES cartridges play with an inexpensive 72-to-60 pin mechanical converter.

1. Why Import from Japan?

The original 1983 red-and-white Famicom only outputs noisy RF video on Japanese TV channels 95/96. The 1993 'AV Famicom' (HVC-101) completely solved this with a standard Nintendo multi-out port (supporting composite and S-Video), redesigned vertical PCB traces eliminating the notorious 'jailbar' interference, and detachable dog-bone ergonomic controllers.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

BOARD REVISION & SHELL INTEGRITY: 1) Model HVC-101 should feature crisp red lettering on the cartridge door. 2) Check controller ports: unlike the hardwired original Famicom, the AV Famicom uses standard 7-pin NES style ports. 3) Inspect for vertical jailbars in video output—pristine late-production HVC-101 revisions have the lowest video noise floor of any official 8-bit Nintendo hardware.

💡 Insider Tip: Search for sets that include both official 'HVC-102' dogbone controllers. The AV Famicom uses the exact same multi-out AV cables and power specs as the Super Famicom and N64.
