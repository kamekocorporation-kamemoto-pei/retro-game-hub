Hori Fighting Commander (HSJ-12 / HPS-09 6-Face-Button Asymmetric Pad)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free! Compatible with Super Famicom / SNES (HSJ-12) or PlayStation 1 / 2 (HPS-09).

1. Why Import from Japan?

Designed specifically for Street Fighter II and competitive arcade fighters, the Hori Fighting Commander moved all 6 action buttons to the face in an arcade-accurate two-row layout, complete with individual turbo switches and an asymmetric grip designed for tabletop claw grip.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

AUTHENTIC HORI STAMP & TURBO SWITCH HEALTH: 1) Authentic controllers feature the gold/silver foil 'HORI FIGHTING COMMANDER' logo on the front face and official model 'HSJ-12' (SFC) or 'HPS-09' (PS1) on the rear plate. 2) Turbo switches: Verify that all 6 independent turbo sliders click firmly between 'OFF', 'TURBO', and 'AUTO'. 3) D-pad pivot: Check that the cross directional pad has not cracked at the central pivot stem.

💡 Insider Tip: The Fighting Commander remains tournament-legal in many retro fighting circuits and offers vastly superior execution over standard stock gamepads.
