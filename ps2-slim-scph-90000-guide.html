1. Why Import from Japan?

The final evolution of the PS2 (SCPH-90000) completely eliminated the bulky external power brick by engineering an ultra-compact internal power supply into the slim chassis. Japan also received exclusive designer colors like Ceramic White and Satin Silver that never reached US retail.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

GENUINE SONY HOUSING & BIOS DATECODE: 1) Confirm the rear model label reads 'SCPH-90000' with the official Sony silver hologram and a 2-prong figure-8 AC inlet. 2) Inspect the top casing: authentic Sony 90000 units feature a distinctive high-gloss mirrored acrylic stripe running vertically down the left side with immaculate flush seams. 3) Check datecode: models with datecode 8C or later cannot run classic FMCB (Free MCBoot) directly; verify you use 'Fortuna' or 'OpenTuna' softmods via memory card instead.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for 'セラミックホワイト' (Ceramic White) or 'サテンシルバー' (Satin Silver) for the cleanest collector aesthetics.
