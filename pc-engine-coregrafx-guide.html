NEC PC Engine CoreGrafx & CoreGrafx II (PI-TG3 / PI-TG4)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Plays Japanese PC Engine HuCards natively. North American TurboGrafx-16 HuCards can be played using a Turbo EverDrive or pinout adapter.

1. Why Import from Japan?

While the original white PC Engine was RF-only, the CoreGrafx upgrade introduced factory A/V composite output in a compact slate-grey chassis with bright orange (CoreGrafx I) or blue (CoreGrafx II) accents, giving collectors the quintessential chiptune powerhouse.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

AUDIO CAPACITOR & CARD SLOT HEALTH: 1) NEC consoles suffer from aging SMD electrolytic capacitors. Always confirm '音声確認済' (Sound verified)—a faulty audio amp circuit produces faint or distorted sound. 2) Inspect the HuCard slot: authentic pins are gold-plated; verify the protective card slider lock moves smoothly without broken internal guide teeth. 3) Look for model number 'PI-TG3' (CoreGrafx) or 'PI-TG4' (CoreGrafx II) stamped on the rear label.

💡 Insider Tip: Pair your CoreGrafx with an 'EDFX' or 'Super SD System 3' on the rear 69-pin expansion bus to output razor-sharp RGB and play full PC Engine Super CD-ROM2 games without fragile CD hardware!
