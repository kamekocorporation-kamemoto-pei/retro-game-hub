🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Compatible with all Sega Saturn consoles worldwide natively.

1. Why Import from Japan?

The Sega Saturn Virtua Stick Pro (HSS-0130) is the undisputed king of home arcade controllers: a colossal 12-pound, two-player steel desk console molded directly to match Sega's legendary Astro City arcade cabinet. It provides genuine dual-stick arcade perfection for Virtua Fighter, Street Fighter Zero 3, and Radiant Silvergun.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

STEEL CHASSIS RUST & LEVER REPLACEMENT: 1) Underside steel plate: Inspect closely for rust spots or pitting. 2) Arcade levers: Early units used Seimitsu LS-56 style parts; verify smooth circular sweep and rapid snap-back to center. 3) Dual controller cords: Ensure both Saturn terminal plugs and cables are intact.

💡 Insider Tip: Because of its immense weight, Japanese proxy shipping via air or surface must be calculated. The internal wiring harness can be effortlessly upgraded with modern Sanwa Denshi JLF levers and OBSF-30 buttons.
