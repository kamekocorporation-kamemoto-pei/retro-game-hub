Konami Pop'n Music Dedicated Controller (RU018 / RU025 9-Button)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: Compatible with all PS1 and PS2 consoles playing Pop'n Music rhythm titles.

1. Why Import from Japan?

Playing Pop'n Music on a standard gamepad is virtually impossible at higher difficulties. The official Japanese dedicated controller (RU018/RU025) provides the full 9-button dome layout color-coded in yellow, white, green, and blue, delivering authentic arcade rhythm coordination right on your living room table.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

DOME BUTTON MEMBRANES & BASE CRACKS: 1) Test all 9 oversized dome buttons; ensure none stick down after being slapped. 2) Rubber feet: Check underneath to confirm non-slip rubber pads are present. 3) Casing: Inspect the bright yellow ABS plastic base for impact cracks around the button perimeter.

💡 Insider Tip: Pick up Pop'n Music Best Hits or Pop'n Music 7-14 on Japanese PS2. Inserting dense foam or mousepads underneath dampens button slap acoustics significantly for apartment play.
