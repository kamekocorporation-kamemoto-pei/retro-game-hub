Game Boy Micro (20th Anniversary Famicom Edition)

🌍 Region & Language Compatibility: 100% Region-Free (Plays all GBA cartridges globally).

1. Why Import from Japan?

The iconic red-and-gold Famicom 20th Anniversary edition and Mother 3 Deluxe Box were flagship releases in Japan and remain in much cleaner collector condition.

🛡️ Authenticity & Anti-Bootleg Checklist (How to Avoid Fakes)

Aftermarket aluminum shells and fake faceplates flood Western sites. Authentic Japanese Famicom Editions have crisp, laser-etched '20th Anniversary' emblems on the back and a flush-fitting OEM faceplate.

💡 Insider Tip: Check the listing photos for 'フェイスプレート傷なし' (Scratch-free faceplate), as OEM faceplates are the hardest part to replace.
